Techniques to Fix Sleep Issues

Here are some of the neuroscience-based strategies we might use together to improve your sleep.

Rewiring Sleep Habits

Many sleep problems are rooted in deeply ingrained habits. We'll use proven behavioral techniques to help you fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer. Together, we'll identify triggers that disrupt your sleep and replace them with routines that support deep rest.

Calming the Racing Mind

Struggling to quiet your thoughts at night? I'll teach you mental exercises and mindfulness practices that calm your mind and reduce nighttime anxiety, so you can drift off naturally.

Resetting Your Body Clock

Your circadian rhythm controls when you feel sleepy or alert. We'll use light exposure, timing of activities, and other science-backed methods to reset your internal clock, making it easier to fall asleep and wake up refreshed.

Creating a Sleep-Friendly Environment

Your bedroom setup can make or break your sleep. We'll optimize your environment—light, temperature, noise, and more—so your body gets the signals it needs to rest deeply.

Breaking the Cycle of Sleeplessness

The more you worry about sleep, the harder it becomes. I'll show you how to break this cycle with cognitive techniques that reduce sleep-related stress and help you build confidence in your ability to rest.
