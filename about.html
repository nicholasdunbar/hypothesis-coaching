My Story

I didn’t become a coach from a textbook. I became one because I struggled with my own health, got overwhelmed by conflicting advice, and felt like I was being told to follow plans that didn’t fit real human behavior. If you’ve ever felt like you were trying hard but still spinning your wheels, I know that feeling personally.

Before coaching, I worked in medical device engineering. That engineering mindset taught me to ask better questions, test assumptions, and build systems that actually work under pressure. Over time, I realized that what many people need isn’t more information—it’s support translating good science into practical daily habits.

That’s why I started Hypothesis Coaching. I wanted to help people the medical system often can’t fully support on its own: people managing complicated lives and health conditions who need a trustworthy partner. Today I serve the Colorado Front Range in person and offer virtual sessions statewide.

My Approach

I combine CBC and MI in plain English: we look at what’s getting in your way, then design very small, doable next steps that make success easier. Instead of guilt and willpower battles, we use curiosity, structure, and accountability.

As a health coach Denver clients can talk to honestly, I focus on practical behavior design, not perfection. If you’re searching for an ACE certified health coach Colorado residents trust, that’s exactly the standard I hold myself to.
