Working With The Psychology of Food

Let's take a look at a few of the approaches we might take in a session.

Accelerating habit formation

You have a lifetime of habits and they can be hard to change. While it's often said that it takes 21 days to form a new habit, with proven behavioral techniques we can speed up the process. Together, we'll also explore mindset shifts, how you think about food, and much more to support lasting change.

Keeping food pleasurable

Many diet books are packed with healthy recipes, but they rarely cater to your unique tastes or cravings. You might find yourself forcing down meals that don't excite your palate, making it hard to stay motivated. That's why a custom approach is essential. We'll work together to create a plan that fits your preferences, so you can enjoy food that is both healthy and genuinely pleasurable. When you love what you eat, you're far more likely to stick with it and see lasting results.

Making food prep faster

Sure, you could cook fantastic gourmet food that's healthy—but who has the time for that? We will explore how to make food prep faster, so you can enjoy delicious meals in minutes. This is especially important for busy people who want to eat healthy but don't have the time to cook elaborate meals every day. We will find ways to make meal prep quick and easy, so you can focus on what really matters: your health and well-being

Eating less, and yet, feeling full

The more calories you eat, the fuller your become, right? Well actually no. Did you know that food manufacturers design food to supress your sense of fullness? They do this by...

  • Making the food soft
  • Using special chemicals
  • Hidding sugar with salt
  • Making the food instantly disolve in the stomach
  • And many more sneaky techniques

This is why it is so hard to stop eating chips, cookies, and other processed foods. It's not your fault, you have been biohacked—and not in a good way. We will work together to find foods that are delicious and filling, so you can eat less and feel satisfied. For example, certain types of protein are more filling than others.
