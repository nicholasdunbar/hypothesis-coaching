How We’ll Use Diet & Exercise to Support Your Therapy

Here are some of the science-based strategies we might use together to help you manage anxiety and depression:

Building a Mood-Boosting Diet

Certain foods can help regulate neurotransmitters and reduce inflammation linked to depression and anxiety. We’ll create simple, enjoyable meal plans that nourish your brain and body—without restriction or guilt.

Movement for Mental Health

Exercise is a proven way to lift mood and reduce anxiety. I’ll help you find activities you enjoy, so movement becomes a natural part of your routine—not a chore.

Creating Synergy with Your Therapist

I’ll collaborate with your therapist’s recommendations, helping you turn insights from therapy into daily actions. Together, we’ll reinforce positive changes and build habits that last.

Managing Stress & Building Resilience

We’ll use practical tools—like mindful eating, breathwork, and sleep routines—to help you handle stress and bounce back from setbacks.
